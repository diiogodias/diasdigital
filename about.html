About Me

The person behind the work

Hi, I'm Diogo

I'm a UK-based web designer helping small businesses get online with websites that actually work. I started building websites because I spotted a gap — too many small businesses either had no website at all, or one that was outdated, slow and not doing them any favours.

I keep things simple. You tell me what you need, I build it, and you get a site you're proud to share. No jargon, no unnecessary upsells, no disappearing after launch.

Every project is built from scratch — no page builders, no cookie-cutter templates. Clean code, fast loading, mobile friendly.
