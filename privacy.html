Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and look after your personal data.

Dias Digital (“we”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it.

What data is collected

When you submit the contact form on this website, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (if you choose to provide it)

The form may also include optional details such as your business name, the service you are looking for, your budget range, and your message. These are used only to understand and respond to your enquiry.

How your data is used

We use this information to respond to your enquiry only. We do not use it for marketing lists, newsletters, or any other purpose unless you ask us to.

We never sell or share your data

Your personal data is never sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Form submissions

Contact form submissions are processed by Formspree on our behalf so we can receive and reply to your message. You can read Formspree’s privacy policy at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous traffic data, such as pages visited and approximate location. This helps us understand how the site is used. Analytics only runs if you accept cookies. Google’s privacy policy is available at google.com/policies/privacy.

Requesting deletion of your data

You can ask us to delete your personal data at any time by emailing diogo@diasdigital.co.uk. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible.

Legal framework

This policy and our handling of personal data are governed by UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
